An ideal opportunity to acquire a freehold residential investment property comprising 5 extremely well maintained and appointed self-contained apartments arranged over three floors with exceptionally neat and tidy communal hall stairs and landing's. Plus immaculate garden areas and hard landscaped communal rear seating and laundry area.

Annual rental income when fully let will be approximately £45,540, which offers 6.5% based on the asking price.

There is, in addition a large offstreet parking area currently accessed from the rear service lane this we believe, has the potential as a building plot, subject to the necessary planning consents and enhances the opportunities this property presents to the astute purchaser. The front garden area includes a vegetable plot, loaned for Starbeck community use on an informal basis, but retained in the full ownership of the vendor, this land could be reacquainted with the property and thus allow additional access to the front and side from the High Street, subject to the necessary consents.

All apartments are double glazed and have independent gas central heating systems, the property has up to date fire systems including emergency lighting and is gas and electrical safety certified. The building has been well maintained both internally and externally and represents an excellent investment opportunity, with added potential for further rental enhancement.

Positioned on Starbeck High Street this double fronted end of terrace imposing property is located within this thriving and popular neighbourhood, well served by local amenities and public transport links including good road and rail access to Harrogate, Leeds, York and beyond

All mains services are connected to the property.
